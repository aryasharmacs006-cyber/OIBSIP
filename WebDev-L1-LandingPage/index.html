our story

It started with one oven that ran four degrees hot.

The Sugar Cloud began in a small home kitchen in 2021, with a stubborn milk bread recipe and an oven that never quite behaved. Friends started asking for boxes. Then friends of friends.

Today it's still a one-kitchen bakery. Everything is made in small batches, the day you collect it, and packed by hand with ribbon. We'd rather bake less and bake it right.

— Arya, head baker (and dishwasher)

how it's made

Slow on purpose

Four steps, none of them rushed. It's why a puff still crackles when you bite it.

  1. 01

    Weigh & sift

    Every ingredient is weighed to the gram. Flour gets sifted twice — it's the secret to the soft crumb.

  2. 02

    Rest overnight

    Doughs and batters sleep in the fridge for 12 hours. Patience does half the flavour work.

  3. 03

    Bake low & slow

    The oven runs a little cooler than most. Longer bake, golden edges, nothing dry.

  4. 04

    Fill & finish by hand

    Cream goes in just before packing, so shells stay crisp until they reach you.

good to know

Questions,
answered

Something missing? Send us a message and we'll reply within a day.

How do I place an order?

Message us on Instagram or email by 8pm for next-day pickup. Custom cakes need three days' notice.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is free from our kitchen window. Local delivery is available within 6 km for a small fee, on weekends.

Can you work around allergies?

We bake with wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts in one kitchen, so we can't promise allergen-free. Tell us what matters and we'll be honest about what's possible.

How should I store cream puffs?

In the fridge, in the box we gave you, and eat them within a day. Let them sit out for ten minutes first.

Can I order a cake for a party?

Yes. We make 6″ and 8″ cakes. Tell us the date, the number of guests and any colours you love.
